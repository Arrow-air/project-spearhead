# PT1.5 hardware update: MAD lift units and HOBBYWING pusher (21 November 2026)

This page records how the `pt15` profile changed for the new PT1.5 propulsion hardware. The
model itself is described in the [model reference](model_reference.md); the previous baseline is
in the [October update](update_2026_10_14.md) and Flight Dynamics note 0003, whose results are for
the earlier 21 kg KDE configuration. The AeroDB reference density was corrected on 26 November
(see below); the results and SITL tables on this page include that correction.

## Hardware

| | Before (note 0003) | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Lift motor (×4) | KDE5215XF-220 | MAD V8013 PRO 150KV (test sheet: IPE edition) |
| Lift ESC (×4) | KDE-UAS75HVC+ | MAD AMPX 80A (5–14S) |
| Lift propeller (×4) | KDE-CF215-DP 21.5×7.3 | MAD FLUXER PRO 26×7.8 MATT |
| Pusher motor | KDE5215XF-220 or MAD V8013 150KV (undecided) | HOBBYWING PLATINUM 5230SL 380KV |
| Pusher ESC | KDE-UAS75HVC+ or MAD AMPX 80A | MAD AMPX 80A (5–14S) |
| Pusher propeller | Wooden 20×12 (APC 20×12 Sport map) | APC 20×12 Sport (LP20012) |
| Batteries | 12S lift pack, 12S pusher pack | unchanged |
| Test site | Ankara, 1.021 kg/m³ | unchanged |

`pt1_as_built` keeps the KDE hardware it flew with.

## Data and models

**Lift.** MAD publishes a test sheet for exactly this motor, ESC and propeller at 48 V, 25 °C and
sea level (15 points, 30–100 % throttle; `data/propulsion/sources/KV150-V8013IPE.webp`). The FDM
lift lookup has no density input, so `scripts/build_mad_lift_curve.py` fits a static propeller
(Ct and Cq linear in RPM) and the motor/ESC equilibrium to the sheet, then rebuilds the curve at
1.021 kg/m³ for 36.0–50.4 V and 15–100 % throttle. The fit reproduces the sheet within 5 % in
thrust, 7.5 % in current and 2 % in RPM. Rows below 30 % throttle are model extrapolation.

**Pusher.** HOBBYWING's sheet gives the 380KV motor at 29.6 V with three APC propellers.
`scripts/fit_hobbywing_pusher_motor.py` fits the equivalent resistance and no-load current to the
53 points at or below 80 A (duty within 4.8 % of throttle, current RMS 2.3 %). The pusher model
combines them with APC's 20×12 Sport map, as before.

**Output limit.** The 5230SL 380KV is rated for 6–8S. On the 12S pack its no-load speed is about
16,900 RPM, and unlimited full throttle would ask for more than 300 A. A new profile field,
`output_limit`, scales commanded throttle to ESC duty; it stands for the pusher servo endpoint.
The PT1.5 value is 0.4494: `SERVO3_MAX` 1450 µs on the AMPX's 1050–1940 µs range. `SERVO3_MAX`
applies in every flight mode; `THR_MAX` does not limit MANUAL in ArduPlane 4.7.1 (its parameter
description and `mode_manual.cpp`). The SITL configurations use 1050–1450 µs, and loading a
configuration checks the channel against the profile.

The 45 % value keeps the worst case at a full pack (50.4 V, any speed up to 28 m/s) at about
55 A and 7,560 RPM, against the AMPX's 80 A continuous rating and APC's 9,500 RPM Sport limit,
while giving more static thrust than either October option and trimming to 28 m/s.

**Mass and inertia.** The 21.0 kg estimate (KDE-based) becomes 22.0 kg:

| Item | Change per unit | Count | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Lift motor, 544 g against 360 g | +184 g | 4 | +0.74 kg |
| Lift propeller, 69 g against 2 × 25.8 g | +17 g | 4 | +0.07 kg |
| Lift and pusher ESC, AMPX 90 g (without wires) against KDE 114 g (with wires) | taken as 0 | 5 | 0 |
| Pusher motor, 590 g against 360 g | +230 g | 1 | +0.23 kg |
| **Total** | | | **+1.04 kg** |

The increments are added to the inertia as point masses at the rotor and pusher stations
(Ixx +0.25, Iyy +0.44, Izz +0.67 kg·m²). The heavier pusher moves the unballasted CG estimate from
about −0.08 m to about −0.085 m; the simulation CG stays at the −0.040 m target. Weigh PT1.5.

## AeroDB reference density (26 November)

The October AeroDB was exported with a reference density of 1.021 kg/m³, but its CFD cases were
run at 1.092 kg/m³ (Aerodynamics note 0003, corrected in PR #34). The coefficients are now
regenerated from the preserved dimensional loads at 1.092 kg/m³; the FDM still flies at the
Ankara density, 1.021 kg/m³. Aerodynamic loads at a given speed and attitude fall by 6.5 %, so
every trim needs more alpha:

| At 22 m/s, target CG | Before the correction | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Alpha | 1.73° | 2.10° |
| Elevator trim | −18.4° | −18.8° |
| Static margin | +6.4 % | +5.5 % |
| CG window (margin ≥ 5 %, elevator ≥ −20°) | −0.046 to −0.027 m | about −0.042 to −0.033 m |
| Stall speed, untrimmed CLmax of the 25 m/s table, 22 kg | 12.9 m/s | 13.4 m/s |

The static margin falls with alpha in this database, which is why the aft bound moves forward.
The −0.040 m target stays inside the window, 2 mm from its forward (elevator) bound and 7 mm
from its aft (margin) bound; ballast the CG to the target, not forward of it.

## Open items

- Bench test: one lift unit (thrust, current, temperature at the hover point) and the pusher
  with the 20×12 at the limited endpoint, both on 12S.
- Confirm the lift motor edition (sheet: IPE; purchased: PRO).
- Hover current per motor (about 20 A) is close to MAD's 24 A sustainable rating; on a hot day
  or at the end of the pack it will approach it. Monitor motor temperature on the first hover.
- AMPX 80A ESCs need good cooling for their continuous rating; PT1.5 ESC cooling is still open.
- Calibrate the lift ESCs to 1100–1940 µs (`Q_M_PWM_MIN/MAX`) and the pusher ESC to 1050–1940 µs,
  then set `SERVO3_MAX` 1450 µs. Keep `THR_MAX` 100 so ArduPilot's 100 % is the endpoint.
- The back-transition peaks at 20.9° pitch while decelerating from 22 m/s with the 9 October
  parameters. It passed, but watch pitch on the first forward → hover transition.
- The 26-inch discs: radius 0.330 m, inner edge at y ±0.22 m, front disc trailing edge at
  x +0.345 m, rear disc leading edge at x −0.420 m. Check clearances against the wing, booms and
  fuselage on the drawing.
